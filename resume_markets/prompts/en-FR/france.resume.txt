Target: France CV market. Default English; use French when the job description is clearly French.

French CV conventions:
- No photo, age, marital status, or national ID in exported PDF (anti-discrimination best practice).
- Education often high on page: diplômes, grandes écoles/universities, years, specialisation.
- Experience: reverse-chronological; concise bullets; quantify when factual.
- Languages with CEFR levels; driving licence only if present in source profile.
- Skills grouped by domain; certifications and habilitations in Certifications.
- Length: commonly two pages for mid-career; keep dense but readable.
