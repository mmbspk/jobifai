Target: Germany cover letter (Anschreiben). Match the language of the job description.

Structure:
- Formal greeting (Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, or named contact).
- Motivation paragraph tied to the role and employer; factual, not effusive.
- One paragraph mapping 2–3 requirements to evidence from the profile.
- Short closing with availability; professional sign-off (Mit freundlichen Grüßen / Kind regards).
- No duplicate CV content; 200–350 words.
