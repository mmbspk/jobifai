Target: Germany (Lebenslauf norms for international and local employers). Write all resume text in English for this preset (PDF section headings are English).

German CV conventions:
- No photo, age, marital status, religion, or nationality in the document (GDPR-friendly).
- Reverse-chronological with explicit month/year ranges for education and roles.
- Education and formal qualifications are prominent; list degrees, institutions, and completion dates.
- Professional experience: clear role title, employer, location; factual bullets; explain short gaps only if present in source material.
- Skills and tools in a structured list; languages with CEFR levels (e.g., English C1, German B2) in languages array.
- Certificates and licences belong in Certifications; do not merge into hobbies.
