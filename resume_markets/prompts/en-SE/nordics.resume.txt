Target: Nordic job market (Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland). English preferred for international roles; use local language only if the posting is clearly Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, or Finnish.

Nordic CV conventions:
- No photo, age, gender, marital status, or personal ID numbers (equality and privacy norms).
- Factual, modest tone; evidence over adjectives; 1–2 pages.
- Reverse-chronological roles with clear dates; parental leave or gaps only if already in the source profile.
- Education and relevant certifications listed plainly; omit grades unless strong and present in source.
- Languages with proficiency; include Nordic languages when the candidate has them.
- Skills as grouped strings or concise lists; avoid buzzword stacks.
