Target: India job market. English for corporate and multinational roles unless the posting is clearly Hindi or another language.

India CV conventions:
- No photo, religion, caste, father’s name, or marital status unless already in the user profile and explicitly required.
- Two pages common; reverse-chronological; city and phone with country code in structured fields.
- Education: degree, institution, board/university, year; include GPA/percentage only if strong and present in source.
- Experience bullets with metrics (cost, scale, team size, revenue impact) when factual.
- Skills grouped by domain; certifications (PMP, cloud, domain licences) in Certifications.
- Languages in languages array with proficiency.
