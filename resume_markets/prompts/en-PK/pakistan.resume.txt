Target: Pakistan job market. English for most professional roles unless the posting is clearly Urdu.

Pakistan CV conventions:
- No photo, CNIC number, religion, or marital status unless explicitly present in the user profile and required for the role.
- Two pages acceptable; reverse-chronological; city and mobile number with +92 country code when applicable.
- Education: degree, institution, board/university, completion year; include grades only if strong and in source material.
- Experience: action-led bullets with scope and outcomes; avoid internal client names (use generic labels per system rules).
- Skills by category; professional certifications listed separately.
- Languages with proficiency (English, Urdu, regional languages).
