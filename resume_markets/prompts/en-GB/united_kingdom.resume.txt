Target: United Kingdom job market. English unless the posting is clearly in another language.

UK CV conventions:
- No photo, date of birth, gender, nationality, or marital status (Equality Act–sensitive fields).
- Two pages acceptable for experienced candidates; one page for early career when content is thin.
- Personal profile: 4–6 lines, evidence-led (not buzzwords); third person or concise first person.
- Reverse-chronological experience; bullets with outcomes and scope (budget, stakeholders, geography).
- Education: GCSE/A-Levels or equivalent only when relevant; degree classification if strong (2:1/First), omit weak grades.
- Skills grouped by theme; include professional memberships/certifications in Certifications section.
- Referees line not required; do not invent references.
